# Lagrange 1.5.0

## Features

- [e9fbcc1](https://github.com/van-geaux/lagrange-reader/commit/e9fbcc14b38072e63a5e04538ab478503c771ac0) Added EPUB annotation foundation with card actions. ([#59](https://github.com/van-geaux/lagrange-reader/pull/59))
  - EPUB annotations can be created, edited, and deleted, and are queued for server synchronization.
  - Annotation cards expose contextual actions including navigating to the annotated location.
- [eee4711](https://github.com/van-geaux/lagrange-reader/commit/eee4711c5f4a8c0bbcc9ac431142c9ebb1e5b009) Preview-only reader and player mode. ([#61](https://github.com/van-geaux/lagrange-reader/pull/61))
  - Reader and player surfaces are available in a preview-only state.
- [d0f2529](https://github.com/van-geaux/lagrange-reader/commit/d0f2529715e315342689e8994a2b6cbf879bd309) Enhanced audiobook player controls. ([#70](https://github.com/van-geaux/lagrange-reader/pull/70), [#68](https://github.com/van-geaux/lagrange-reader/issues/68))
  - Full-screen player with chapter previous/next and seeking, skip intervals, speed, sleep timer, and lock mode.
  - Session history and cover viewing are integrated into the player.
  - Responsive portrait and landscape layouts, preserved browser route, shared chapter sheet, centered seek labels, and marquee titles.
- [ccc87f1](https://github.com/van-geaux/lagrange-reader/commit/ccc87f162c45a9c203d2770e67ca6974e6e2e36f) Added background-safe book downloads. ([#84](https://github.com/van-geaux/lagrange-reader/pull/84), [#77](https://github.com/van-geaux/lagrange-reader/issues/77))
  - Downloads continue when the app moves to the background or the screen turns off.
  - Progress and completion notifications are shown in the system notification shade.
- [bf10ab8](https://github.com/van-geaux/lagrange-reader/commit/bf10ab810dc9a468dc4d9c9be5b1dfdbb600363d) Improved fullscreen cover viewer. ([#85](https://github.com/van-geaux/lagrange-reader/pull/85), [#73](https://github.com/van-geaux/lagrange-reader/issues/73))
  - Cover is centered on the physical display and fitted to its aspect ratio.
  - Pinch/pan, double-tap zoom, and tap reset gestures are supported.
  - Tapping outside the cover or pressing Back dismisses the viewer.
  - The displayed cover can be saved through platform storage APIs.
