# Lagrange 2.2.0

Release date: 2026-11-26

This release adds native Grimmory support and in-app APK updates, expands audiobook controls in Android Auto, and makes reader preferences and EPUB navigation more flexible.

## Highlights

- Connect to Grimmory with native account, catalog, reading, download, and progress support, and update the app by downloading a compatible release APK from within Lagrange. ([PR #440](https://github.com/van-geaux/lagrange-reader/pull/440), [PR #439](https://github.com/van-geaux/lagrange-reader/pull/439)) ([`van-geaux`](https://github.com/van-geaux), [`van-geaux`](https://github.com/van-geaux))
- Customize reader settings across libraries, keep them associated with the same server and account, and open EPUB footnotes without losing your place. ([PR #404](https://github.com/van-geaux/lagrange-reader/pull/404), [PR #434](https://github.com/van-geaux/lagrange-reader/pull/434), [PR #427](https://github.com/van-geaux/lagrange-reader/pull/427)) ([`JimmyJi0309`](https://github.com/JimmyJi0309), [`JimmyJi0309`](https://github.com/JimmyJi0309), [`JimmyJi0309`](https://github.com/JimmyJi0309))

## A note from the developer (you can skip this section)

**TL;DR:** Lagrange is continuing. BookOrbit’s official Android app is here, and we’re still committed to building the reader we set out to make.

Lagrange exists for one very specific reason: when I started it, there was no Android reader for BookOrbit, the book server I used then and still use today. I had never developed an Android app before. Seeing Lagrange grow from that first attempt into the app it is now makes me incredibly proud, and I owe so much of that to the contributors and everyone who reports issues and helps us improve it. I love you guys.

A few weeks ago, BookOrbit released the beta of its official Android app, and I absolutely love it. It is more polished than I could have imagined Lagrange being at this stage. Lagrange now supports other book servers as well, but BookOrbit is still at its heart. It makes complete sense that many people will choose the official app, and I’m genuinely happy to see such a good reader arrive for the server that inspired this project.

Lagrange began as a companion for BookOrbit, and that connection still matters to me. Over time, it has grown to support other self-hosted book servers too. That growth doesn’t change where Lagrange came from; it lets us serve more readers across the self-hosted book community. We’ll keep improving the app for BookOrbit users and for everyone else who chooses Lagrange. I don’t know whether we’ll ever match the polish of BookOrbit’s official app, but we’ll keep striving to make Lagrange better. Development will continue.
