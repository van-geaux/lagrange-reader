# Lagrange 1.5.4

First of all, good news for everyone: the BookOrbit maintainer has confirmed that they are hard at work on an official BookOrbit Android client. You can follow the update in [this issue comment](https://github.com/bookorbit/bookorbit/issues/1269#issuecomment-5774780906). I really cannot wait to see it.

What does this mean for Lagrange? Lagrange will continue to be maintained and will not be abandoned. At the same time, support for other book servers is now part of the roadmap, including Audiobookshelf, Komga, Kavita, CWA, and others. The goal is to support those servers and bring the Lagrange reading and listening experience to them as well.

Do we have a timeline? Not really. The plan and preparation work are already underway, so stay tuned.

## Others

- Lagrange 1.5.4 continues to support BookOrbit's dedicated audiobook API introduced in BookOrbit v2.10.0.
- The default release download uses the combined APK name `Lagrange-1.5.4.apk`.
- Architecture-specific alternatives use `Lagrange-1.5.4-x86.apk`, `Lagrange-1.5.4-x86_64.apk`, and `Lagrange-1.5.4-arm.apk` when published.
- If you are unsure which architecture to choose, download the plain combined APK.
- Native AppAuth/Custom Tabs remains deferred until BookOrbit provides deployed mobile-redirect support.
- MOBI, AZW, AZW3, and FB2 remain unsupported.

## Future Android distribution plans

Android is tightening restrictions around sideloaded applications, so we are considering releasing Lagrange on the Google Play Store.

Will Lagrange be a paid app? Yes and no:

- The GitHub APK will always remain free of charge.
- The Play Store version is planned as a one-time, low-cost purchase for the convenience provided by the Play Store environment, including automatic updates and app verification.
- Both versions will be exactly the same. No features or functions will be locked behind the Play Store version.

We will not start this process soon. The current plan is to consider Play Store distribution only after Lagrange supports some servers other than BookOrbit. Advanced users who are comfortable sideloading an unverified APK will be able to continue using the free GitHub version, while the Play Store option will be available for users who prefer its installation and update experience.
